Arcane Rush
A one-thumb lane shooter for iPhone. Your apprentices fire on their own — your job is the road. Offline, no ads, nothing to buy.

The game

Drag. Fire. Break the horde.

Drag your thumb and the squad follows it, one lane wide and growing as it grows. Every gate you walk through is a choice: the one that doubles you, or the one that was closer. Ahead, the road fills with skeletons, chargers and shielded brutes, and at the end of every road something bigger is waiting.

What's inside

  • Forty levels across two roads — the meadow road and Frostfell — and two bosses.
  • The Academy: upgrade the squad, pick an ember, storm or frost staff with three evolutions each, and raise a wisp that hunts beside you.
  • A bestiary that pays: hunt a kind enough times and the Wardrobe opens new colours.
  • Three daily missions, a streak, and a seeded endless mode.

One player, offline, finished

No adverts, no in-app purchases, no account, and nothing about you leaves your phone.
