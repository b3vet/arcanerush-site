Privacy Policy

Arcane Rush collects no data

Arcane Rush does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, no crash reporting, no in-app purchases, and no third-party SDKs that gather information.

No network requests

The game runs fully offline. Everything it shows — art, sound, levels — is bundled inside the app, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps one save on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage. It holds:

  • the level you have reached and your coins;
  • your upgrades, staff, wisp and wardrobe choices;
  • bestiary counters, daily missions and your streak;
  • two settings: sound on/off and the performance panel on/off.

This save never leaves your device. We have no copy of it. Deleting the app deletes it permanently.

Children

Because the app collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children.

Contact

If you email us for support, we only see the information you choose to include in that message, and use it solely to reply to you. Privacy questions: berkeucvet@gmail.com.
