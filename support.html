Frequently asked

How do I play?

Your squad of apprentice wizards fires on its own. You steer: drag your thumb left and right and the squad follows. Walk through the gates that grow your squad, avoid the ones that shrink it, and break every number before it reaches you.

  • DragMove the squad across the road
  • GatesWalk through to add to, multiply or lose from your squad
  • AcademySpend coins from clears on the squad, the staff and the wisp

There's no sound

Check the mute button on the Academy screen or in the run's HUD — the setting is remembered between sessions.

What is the panel with numbers on it?

Triple-tapping the title on the title screen (or the level chip during a run) shows a small performance panel with frame rate and draw calls. It changes nothing about the game; triple-tap again to hide it.

Does it work offline?

Yes — Arcane Rush is fully offline. There is no login and no internet connection is needed; everything the game uses is inside the app.

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress — level reached, coins, upgrades, bestiary and settings — is saved only on your device. There are no accounts, so progress does not move between devices, and deleting the app deletes it.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. Arcane Rush has no advertising and nothing to buy. Coins are earned by playing and are only spent inside the game.
